Installation Guide

01

Download & Extract

Download the latest release archive velocityx-chrome-v1.0.0.zip and unpack it into a persistent local directory.

02

Navigate to Extensions

Open your Chromium browser and go to your extensions dashboard:

chrome://extensions
03

Enable Developer Mode

Switch on the Developer mode toggle located in the upper-right corner of the Extensions page.

04

Load Unpacked Extension

Click the Load unpacked button and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.

01

Download Firefox Archive

Download velocityx-firefox-v1.0.0.zip to your machine.

02

Open Debugging Console

Navigate to Firefox's internal add-on debugger:

about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox
03

Load Temporary Add-on

Click Load Temporary Add-on... and choose either the downloaded ZIP archive or the extracted manifest.json file.

All permissions are strictly scoped to active tabs with HTML5 video elements.
